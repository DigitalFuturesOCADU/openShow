This event is where we show the work that is happening **RIGHT NOW**. OPEN comes from the fact that this call is open to our whole community. The show will include works from current Digital Futures undergraduate students, graduate students, faculty, and alumni. For anyone who has asked the question “What IS Digital Futures anyway?” - this project showcase will help to provide some answers. The collection of work presented is intended to represent a survey of current ideas, concepts, themes, theories, tools, techniques, and trends being explored by the Digital Futures community. This year’s OPEN show will take place on **December 7, 6-8 pm in 205 Richmond St W**. We look forward to seeing you and joining our amazing community event to explore the future!   ![](../images/2023/11/Screenshot-2023-11-22-171449-198x300.png)
