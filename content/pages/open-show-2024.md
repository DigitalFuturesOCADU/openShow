## DIGITAL FUTURES OPEN SHOW **2025** is happening...

### _on_ Thursday, **December 11th**

### _from_ **5:00 to 8:00 PM**

### _at_ **205 Richmond Street West**

### _in_ **the Graduate Gallery, Experimental Media Space, and rooms 115, 118, 120**.

This event is where we show the work that is happening **RIGHT NOW**. "OPEN" comes from this call being open to our whole community. Presented works represent a survey of ideas, concepts, themes, theories, tools, techniques, and trends explored in the last year by the Digital Futures community, including Digital Futures **undergraduate students**, **graduate students**, **faculty**, and **alumni**. For anyone who has asked the question “What IS Digital Futures anyway?” – this project showcase will help to provide some answers.

#### see you there!
