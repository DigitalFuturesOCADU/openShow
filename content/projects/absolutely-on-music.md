Absolutely on Music is an experiment in Zero UI (invisible interfaces). The project incorporates Haruki Murakami and Seiji Ozawa's book "Absolutely on Music" where the author and conductor have on going conversations about music. The book itself is a tangible interface that plays the music mentioned in the book in each chapter from speaker placed below an interactive chair that lights up a reading lamp when a participant sits down.
