AR Disclosures (WIP) is a prototype of part of a graduate thesis project that explores the use of new media as a storytelling tool in the fight against HIV. The prototype is an augmented reality poster that uses latrinalia i.e toilet grafitti - designs to speak on issues like stigma, discrimination, and more in relation to the AIDS crisis.
