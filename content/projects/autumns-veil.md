Autumn’s Veil is an immersive and interactive installation that encapsulates the beauty and transience of seasonal change. Blending art and technology to reflect the inevitability of life’s cycles, this installation’s central message is that change is inevitable and reminds observers to nurture their inner light through the good times and the trying periods, or seasons, in life.
