"Between the Cards" is an interactive experience inspired by tarot cards. The project aims to provide a calming, meditative, and introspective experience that explores topics about life, what it means to exist, and future possibilities through tarot-inspired landscapes and characters. Interact with the characters to uncover their story while exploring the low-poly environment. The current version explores one card: The Fool.
