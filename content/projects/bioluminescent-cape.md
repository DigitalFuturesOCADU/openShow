The Bioluminescent Cape is a cape that borrows design from an armour piece in from the game: Monster Hunter World Iceborne. The cape is also inspired by the bioluminescent properties of a jellyfish and how energy is transferred from food and environment into light. The Bioluminescent Cape uses solar panels to charge up two rechargeable batteries hidden in the cape, and when the light level is low, the energy is released, powering up two light strings hidden in the cape, lighting up the tendrils of the cape.
