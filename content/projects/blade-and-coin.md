For decades rivaling pacts of adventurers have roamed the land, pulling off many daring heists of loot from holds across the country. Recently, many mythical Dragons and their hoards have been discovered, with untold fortunes being available to those who dare challenge these fearsome beasts. In this board game, you and fellow fighters race to capture as much treasure from these dragons as possible, ready to face danger head on.
