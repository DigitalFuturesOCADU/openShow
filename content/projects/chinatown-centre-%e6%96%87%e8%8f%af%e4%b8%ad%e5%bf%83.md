"CHINATOWN CENTRE is an interactive VR exploration of liminal space. Using several LiDAR scans, audio tracks, and text panels this piece puts the player in the place of the explorer, letting them freely walk through the space at their own pace while providing atmospheric and situational ambience. 
文華中心 是对阈限空间的交互式 VR 探索。使用多个 LiDAR 扫描、音轨和文本面板，这件作品将玩家置于探险者的位置，让他们按照自己的节奏自由地穿越空间，同时提供氛围和情境氛围。"
