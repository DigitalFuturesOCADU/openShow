"Coming All Together is a digital video that weaves together personal recordings from different individuals, highlighting the unique experiences that shape their lives. Each clip stands on its own yet becomes part of a larger story, revealing the richness and complexity of human existence.

The piece invites viewers to reflect on the moments that define us and consider how, when brought together, they form a tapestry of shared and individual experiences. Through its visuals and music, made from sounds recorded from real-life moments, it offers a new way to see into someone else’s life, fostering a deeper appreciation for the personal and intimate nature of each experience. In this space, everyone’s experiences merge in a unique way, allowing for a simultaneous connection across time and perspective."
