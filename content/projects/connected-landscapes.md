Connected Landscapes explores the intersection of technology and craft by creating electronic tapestries. Inspired by my grandmother’s rug hooking, the work reimagines traditional textiles using electronic materials, drawing inspiration from Canadian landscapes to explore themes of connectivity, cultural identity, and the impact of digital networks.
