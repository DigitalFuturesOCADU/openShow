"The Game of Life is a cellular automaton devised by mathematician John Conway in 1970 that stimulates the evolution of simple life cells based on a set of rules: cells survive, die, or multiply depending on their neighbouring conditions.

Watch how the installation explores these patterns of life and evolution, where every cell is in a cycle of survival and decay. Use the screen to introduce new lifeforms and watch it shape or destroy the community.
"
