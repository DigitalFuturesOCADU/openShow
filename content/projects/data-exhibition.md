"Data Exhibition" is a VR exhibition about the overuse of cameras and the data collection of people.  For example, phones, laptops, iPad, roads, security purpose cameras, stores, markets, etc. And your whereabouts and behaviour are being recorded and easy to find out. And in many cases, whether or not the camera is on, all aspects of data are tracked and recorded, and the basic preferences of the person's information have been recorded. By creating some 3D models and exhibiting them in the virtual environment, I would like to let everyone enter the world and get an immersive experience. The models' shapes are different from the real world, which enhances the feeling of being recorded.
