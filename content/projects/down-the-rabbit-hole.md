Down the Rabbit Hole is an experiential VR piece that allows the viewer to explore objects from their childhood through touch while floating in a mysterious tunnel. The farther you are in the tunnel, the younger you become, landing in your childhood bedroom. Taking inspiration from Alice in Wonderland, the same objects the user interacted with are placed carefully in the bedroom, allowing the user to explore and reminisce on their past.
