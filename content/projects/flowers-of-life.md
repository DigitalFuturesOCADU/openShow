As the second iteration of a previous project originally named Life. In the previous iteration, the controller was connected to a computer that feeds it data simulating numbers of people born and died in each of the 6 continents and the world as a whole. The data is then reflected on 14 motors with each pair representing Life and Death in each continent. The purpose of this kinetic sculpture is to celebrate the constant cyclical movement of life and death as part of this experience we call Life.
