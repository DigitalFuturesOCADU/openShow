Food Truck Story (working title) is our first studio project. It is an XR culinary life-sim game where the player runs their very own food truck business. Meet unique customers and immerse yourself in the surrounding city. Players will dabble with cooking mechanics that pay homage to nostalgic cooking games from our childhood like the Cooking Mama or the Papa Louie franchises, all while introducing new technology through virtual reality motion-based gameplay.
