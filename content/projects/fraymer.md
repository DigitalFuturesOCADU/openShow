"Fraymer is a motion graphics platform that makes animation accessible to anyone—no prior experience needed. Our Figma plugins, Flyp and Splyt, bring motion directly into designers’ workflows.

Flyp allows anyone to create simple but flexible frame-by-frame animations, like a digital flipbook, producing smooth, professional-quality sequences effortlessly. Splyt takes this further, enabling precise animation from just 1 design for more expressive, complex motion

Together, the plugins transform static designs into dynamic storytelling, blending computational design with creative expression. Users can experiment, iterate quickly, and unlock playful, professional motion in ways previously inaccessible.

This work embodies the spirit of Digital Futures, demonstrating how technology can amplify human creativity. By focusing on intuitive, thoughtful tools, we empower designers to bring ideas to life and reimagine what animation can accomplish in everyday design workflows."
