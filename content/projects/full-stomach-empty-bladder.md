"Full Stomach, Empty Bladder follows a woman trying to free her younger self from the shackles of her family that she doesn’t get along with.

She enters a dreamlike manifestation of her family home within her subconscious, and finds her schoolgirl self, having grown big enough to burst out the house physically, but still literally chained to it.

Can she find her way around that nightmarish house and break free? Play minigames that represent various aspects of control that her family exercised on her in order to break the chains they placed on her when she was younger. "
