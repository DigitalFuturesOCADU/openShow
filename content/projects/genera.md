Play in a Virtual Reality world as an android sent to a far away planet, conducting research on the native flora. In total isolation, you tend to the various plants in your greenhouse outpost, the only android at this station. Headquarters regularly checks in and gives you tasks each day, and there are many plants for you to take care of. You work to plant, feed and maintain them. Keeping them healthy is your main priority. Although understaffed, it is a peaceful and beautiful planet to spend your time on, albeit frustrating. The plants are demanding and HQ expects results.
