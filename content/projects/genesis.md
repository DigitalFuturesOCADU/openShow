Since the Covid hit, our R&amp;D team has been working on exploring a new form of art practice to engage an online interactive experience that bridges the performers and the performance in a different space synchronously to create a remote laser-projection mapping show in real-time. The visuals of this project is an attempt to abstractly explore and manifest concepts and mythological references in themes of cosmogony, through contemporary mediums and creative coding.

It's been a wonderful experience and an honour to collaborate with Associate professor Adam Tindale and such incredible teammates to push the boundaries of this project and grounded it successfully.  Unfortunately, due to the restriction in Toronto, Canada. We're not allowed to gather the audience to appreciate the full live performance onsite. However, if we get the opportunities anytime soon, we will spare no effect to push this to happen again.
