Hero Corpse is a 2D pixel action-adventure platformer. Players will control the corpse of a failed hero and explore a high-fantasy world of unconventional creatures and environments expressed through detailed yet clear pixel art, inspired by the classic hand-painted anime style. It will attempt to take a new approach to the traditional fantasy genre by combining elements of older western fantasy and traditional Japanese culture.
