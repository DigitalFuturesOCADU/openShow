A bullet hell game contained in a website using P5.js. The story of the game has you play as Clarisse, a hunter from the Auren plains, hunting down a mischievous fox causing fires in farmers' fields. The goal of the game is to dodge the fox's bullets and storm through his patterns to claim victory.

Project contains three modes of difficulty. Will you take it easy like a training exercise or will you weather through the blazing heat?
