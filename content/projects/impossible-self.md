"Impossible Self" is an experiment of volumetric capture. The figure was volumetrically captured and putting them in Unity to add effects and environment. The concept would be that we are in a multidimensional world, and in each world, we have our own identities and figures that live there and sometimes the figures will act the same in a different world. Since the multidimensional is the imagery of the world, it is not able to confirm that it exists in our real life. Also, it will not happen if the world exists so many same figures as us doing the same things. Thus, all these elements would be considered impossible to happen which fits the theme of impossible selves.
