"“Into the Unknown” beckons explorers to a corner veiled in white fabric, concealing a realm of mystery. This interactive installation transforms the unnoticed corner into a magic experience. As visitors approach, the fabric and light respond with subtle undulations, hinting at the unseen wonders beneath. Merging reality with imagination. ""Into the Unknown"" transcends the ordinary, inviting individuals to linger, contemplate, and become part of an enigmatic journey where the familiar transforms into the extraordinary.

The installation starts as an inconspicuous corner covered with white fabric with a shadow projection. There are no apparent signs indicating anything special within.
As someone approaches the installation, a built-in camera detects the presence, and the white fabric begins to produce subtle undulations as if some form of energy is flowing within. when detects people's movements, the light will start to follow people's movements and create a dynamic shadow. When the user leaves the sensing area, the fabric gently folds back and the light stops moving to its initial state, ready to welcome the next explorer.
"
