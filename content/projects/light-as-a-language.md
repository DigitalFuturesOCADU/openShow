"This installation uses neo pixels and a simple button to create a “dialogue mediator” among users. Light becomes a tool to communicate, either with the outside spectators or inside, other users. Each press to the button will light up a single pixel. When there is no pixel left to light up, a small show of lights will appear and then the cycle will repeat itself.
"
