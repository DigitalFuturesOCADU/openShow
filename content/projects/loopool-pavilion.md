Loopool is a graceful ring structure. It is inspired by traditional Chinese natural landscapes. Its structure is wound into two loops like a snake and supports the structure itself, which also symbolizes infinity. As a pavilion, it separates a small space from the public space and provides people with various uses, such as a public stage or a place to play chess.
