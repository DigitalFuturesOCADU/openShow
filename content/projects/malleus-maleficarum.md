Malleus Maleficarum is a story-driven puzzle video game where you play as someone appointed by the church to hunt down and arrest witches. The game happens in 7 in-game days, each lasting 10 minutes. The goal of the player is to correctly arrest 20 witches in those 7 days to win the game. Throughout the game you will be confronted with moral decisions and ethical problems, and decide the fate of you and many others by the ed of the game.
