"Memory Map is a digital network of points floating in space, capturing the essence of Toronto’s landscape. The project explores the connections between architecture, memory, and perception, transforming the city into a fragmented, dreamlike world. Toronto’s solid structures dissolve into a hazy, vaporous form, blurring the line between the physical city and its digital reflection.

The point cloud in Memory Map represents fleeting moments, like pieces of a dream stored in the digital realm. These scattered points shows how we remember cities in dreams: incomplete, shifting, and timeless.

This project encourages viewers to consider how our experience of the city could feel like navigating a database, where everything is interconnected, but we can never fully grasp the bigger picture. It’s a world suspended between what we remember and what we imagine, constantly changing and never fully fixed.
