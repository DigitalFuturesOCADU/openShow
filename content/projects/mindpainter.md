Have you ever seen your thoughts? Did you know how beautiful they are? MindPainter takes readings from your brainwaves and interprets them into fantastical abstract imagery. Each image is one-of-a kind, unique to your mind and the moment it was created. Save your painting with the touch of a button and share your mind's creation with your friends.
