An application using geolocation-based AR technology to create time capsules that host people’s personal memories at specific locations where those experiences happened. The application allows people to revisit their past memories triggered by the contextual information: location. Reminiscing as an important part of human behavior helps people to develop a strong self-concept, enhance the social bond and improve their future thinking. This project aims to explore how Extend Reality technology could support reminiscing within the context of the flourish in XR industry.
