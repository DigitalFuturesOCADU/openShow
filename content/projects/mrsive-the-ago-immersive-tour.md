MRsive - The AGO Immersive Tour - is an MR/AR activated solution for indoor wayfinding within complex architectural spaces such as museums, airports, hospitals etc...   The MRsive project is part of Jad Rabbaa's thesis and research, currently in the development and user-testing stage being conducted at the Art Gallery of Ontario - AGO. The MRsive project not only explores how might Augmented Reality make wayfinding experience at the AGO more intuitive but also implements different AR multisensory interactions to engage visitors with the different artifacts in the space.
