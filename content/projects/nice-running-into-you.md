An anthropomorphic phantom engages unsuspecting passers-by in a game of peek-a-boo. Using webcam detection to track users and projection as output, the interaction pans out like a playful confrontation, taking from the realms of the unexpected and otherworldly right out to the corner of an everyday sidewalk.
