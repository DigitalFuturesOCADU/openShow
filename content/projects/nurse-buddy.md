Conventional nurse buzzers in hospital rooms are obscure and usually consist of just a single button. This often leads to alarm fatigue for nurses and hampers the efficiency of the healthcare provision process within hospital environments. Nurse Buddy is a wearable electronic textile wristband designed for patients, aimed at reforming conventional nurse buzzers. It allows patients to send more focused messages with precise requirements to nurse stations in order to help nurses function more optimally. The prototype has been designed for both the patient and nurse in an attempt to improve levels of comfort and empathy in otherwise intimidating and often sterile hospital environments for patients and to reduce fatigue for nurses. The patient prototype has been created used multiple textile techniques, combined with soft circuits and haptic feedback mechanisms. It is supported by a browser-based interface and auditory feedback for nurses and aims to provide a seamless experience for both users. The system has been built to improve nurse-patient communication and to give both users the respect they deserve while simultaneously streamlining the provision of care in a healthcare environment.
