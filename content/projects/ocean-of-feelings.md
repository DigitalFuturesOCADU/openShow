Ocean of Feelings is a two-level narrative exploration game that uses the ocean as a metaphor for emotional depth. The player controls a curious fish who dives into unknown waters in search of meaning, only to encounter creatures who are struggling with their own emotional challenges. As the player travels deeper, the environments darken, the atmosphere shifts, and the creatures' stories become more complex, mirroring the journey of confronting difficult feelings.
Each NPC represents a different emotional state. The lonesome jellyfish grapples with alienation and the desire to belong. Mr. Angler, a grieving widower, searches endlessly for his lost partner in the darkness. The electric eel struggles with uncontrolled emotions that harm the people around him. Through dialogue choices and small quests, players help these characters face what they fear while uncovering parts of themselves along the way. These choices determine the player's path, shaping multiple endings and encouraging reflection on empathy and emotional care.
Developing ""Ocean of Feelings"" in only seven weeks taught our team how to merge design, coding, writing, and emotional storytelling under real creative constraints. We worked across disciplines to build a meaningful prototype, and although we had to cut one planned layer near the end of production, the process reflects the nature of collaborative game-making on a tight timeline. Even without the ""happy"" ending we hoped to include, the game captures the emotional spirit at its core: empathy, vulnerability, and the courage to explore one's inner world.
