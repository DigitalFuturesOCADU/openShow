"Gravity is a fundamental force of nature, responsible for holding planets in orbit, keeping us on the ground, and even forming the atmosphere and oceans of Earth.  
Orbs Interactive Light uses a DIY pressure sensor, the gravity force, and glass orbs to shape this playful interactive light.
The main materials for the product are cardboard and paper, making it fully recyclable.
Nature, natural forces, astronomy, and the visible or hidden bonds they have were the primary source of inspiration for this project. 

Interactive designs that utilize natural forces and principles to create a playful experience were the other source of inspiration for this project. 
"
