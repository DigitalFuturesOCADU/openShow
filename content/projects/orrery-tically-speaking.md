‘Orrery-tically Speaking’ is a project about our memories 
and the use of space and time to orient them. Through 
this thesis project, I am interested in exploring how time can be presented in an unfamiliar display that can become 
an association of a memory or an anticipated event in the future. I am building an orrery (model solar system) that relies on data gathered though astrometry to allow viewers to see what the solar system looked like on a given day
