OurOS is a physical game for rural community members to help identify problems in their vicinity and establish clear goals for building a sustainable community. Game players would be able to explore the current state of education, health, sanitation, environment and finance in their community, and thereafter they would be able to create a plan of action that is widely understood and acknowledged by all members of the community.
