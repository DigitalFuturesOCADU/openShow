Paint Town is a Unity multiplayer sandbox web game all about painting, exploring and playing in a wonderous environment. Players can explore the town with friends and paint on walls, as well as run, jump, and parkour around the buildings. Minigames such as hangman and tic tac toe are scattered around the map, reverse tag can also be started by spraying the white box, where players compete to be the last one "it" before the timer runs out.
