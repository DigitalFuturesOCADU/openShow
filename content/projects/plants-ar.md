This AR experience provides a natural escape to the world of plants. I love green, and the environment and plants. I was very proud of what I had created, it was truly a fun experience when creating my first AR. At first, I thought it was going to be difficult but when creating it with Onirix, going through the tutorials, sending it to family and friends to play around with it. I was very impressed with what I had created. Unfortunately, I had only a 10-day trial so now the code has expired, so I hope the video is sufficient. When opening Onirix, It will tell you to find an open area on the ground and the objects will appear. When clicking on the sunflower, it will generate sound, sadly that did not work for folks. You can move the objects around, take a picture, re-size them. You can even go all around the landscape to get a more close-up visual. Overall, since this was my first time creating an AR experience, I decided to make it more simplistic and straightforward. The thing with Onirix was that it did not require coding. You just create everything in the Onirix studio. I am happy and proud of myself for creating this work and I will probably create more in the future.
