Project Easter is a modular interactive learning tool based in TouchDesigner using an Arduino with 4 Push buttons. Easter specifically aims at teaching users about Easter Island and Moai( the prominent stone figures on Easter Island). It does this by “waking up the Moai” and telling the user a fact when a button is pressed and held. Easter is designed to have its subject matter switched out to teach different topics, and be installed in different places like museums or learning centers. The primary audience for Easter would be children or people with learning differences as a fun and interactive way to learn.
