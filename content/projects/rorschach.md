Rorschach is a visual experience that invites viewers to gaze and contemplate. Inspired by the Rorschach Test, the animated imagery are designed to draw out underlying thoughts. The randomly generated forms unfold in hypnotic sweeping motions, allowing time for viewers to recognize, relate, and attribute value to the imagery.  
  
Visuals are created using coded algorithms with p5 Javascript and HTML. Displayed on a CRT TV with a raspberry pi 3 B+.
