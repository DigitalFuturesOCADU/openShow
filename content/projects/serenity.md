"Today, I am choosing Serenity."

This is a generative art made with Touch Designer and Touch OSC. It was for Atelier I, Experiment 2. I wanted to create generative art that is calm, abstract, and interactive. By implementing Touch OSC, it gives you the ability to morph and change the components in Touch Designer. More information and video in the link provided. 

This generative art is called Serenity because the movement of the art brings you inner peace as it freely glides around the space. The interactive part allows you to be in control of that peace, removing the chaos in your life just for a few moments. Serenity will help you stay calm and true to yourself.
