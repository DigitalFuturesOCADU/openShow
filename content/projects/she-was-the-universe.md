"When the whale falls, everything grows. The endless cycle of life after death consists of our past, present, and future.
We metaphorically refer to this fish-like creature as the universe, which swims independently and does not react to the outside world. When people walk by it, they will see different shapes inside it: snails, corals, and humanoid creatures. These figures represent past and future life and consciousness."
