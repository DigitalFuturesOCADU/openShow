This environmental installation of the covered walk pathway and the double cylinders (tubes) allowing people to watch the night sky. Apart from the inside cylinder and the top cone parts made of transparent glass, the outer and inner surfaces are built with metallic material, creating a robust future sense. They are aiming to make connections with the universe and space. People can walk through from the front entrance, then cross the long sidewalk to the inner double cylinder area, enjoying the mixture visual experience of both the technology-inspired artificial space and the vast natural starry landscape.
