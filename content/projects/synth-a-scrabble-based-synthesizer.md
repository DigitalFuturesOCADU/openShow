SYNTH lets you make music from words! This tool can help you learn the basic building blocks of synthesis: carrier waves, envelopes, filters, and modulation. Depending on the tile's position, letter, and neighbours, a tile either makes a sound or modifies its nearest sound. See below for a demo video.

Try the tool here: https://editor.p5js.org/Grgmrtn/present/uLPi_vr7h

Built using p5.js (https://p5js.org/) and flocking.js (https://flockingjs.org/).
