"Take a  
"" invites you to pause, reflect, and engage with your digital alter ego. As the lines between our physical being and our digital persona become increasingly blurred, it's a gentle reminder that our online traces are just an extension of our real-life selves. These digital doppelgängers play a huge part in how we navigate and make sense of the digital world. This piece encourages introspection about the differences and similarities between our digital selves and our tangible existence. By facing these dual aspects, our goal is to make sense of what it means to exist in this digital age. "
