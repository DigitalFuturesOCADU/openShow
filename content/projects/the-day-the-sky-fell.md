What do you do when it’s the last day on Earth, but you barely feel like you’ve experienced anything? The Day the Sky Fell is a soft apocalyptic adventure-exploration game, set in near future Tokyo, the day the Earth’s atmosphere suddenly crumbles and gravity flips, upending the world as we know it. Play a stubborn young woman determined to live her last day to its fullest—starting with a childhood trip to Tokyo Tower that never happened.
