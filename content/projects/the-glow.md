The Glow is an audio-visual installation based around Craig Reynold's Boids program designedto emulate the flocking behaviour of birds. The physical canopy is designed to immerse audience members as they enter/exit the building via the Duncan Street doorway. As they travel up or down the stairway the LED patterns react to their footsteps while the audio generated by the piece simultaneously warps.
