"The No Man's Zone in Desert IV" is an adventure narrative game that explores in the form of the text-based, 3D game, and AR. The text-based version is built in Twine, the 3D version is built in Unity, and the AR form is built in Adobe Aero. The 3D version is still in a developed status, only including the beginning of the narrative. Applying various gaming mechanisms in the text-based version is very interesting. The player will be the adventurer in the game that needs to go through different strange and mysterious events during the adventure. And they need to select the choice during the game to go to the end of the story. The story ends may vary based on the selection that they made.
