"The Rage Room is a VR destruction simulator experience! Taking place in an open canyon, use your VR hand controllers as punching gloves as you destroy pillars of rocks leaving pieces in your wake with realistic physics. Featuring a low poly art-style and a simple premise, the only thing you need to focus on is releasing your energy. Created using Unity and the Normcore plugin, this project was designed for emotional release and overall fun. The Rocks split and respond to the strength of your punches in real time, allowing you to get creative with your movements to see what kind of mayhem can be wrought!
"
