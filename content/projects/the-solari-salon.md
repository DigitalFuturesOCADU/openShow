“Saddle up, pardner, and step into the charm of our hotel parlour room, a slice of the Old West brought to life with a tech twist. Adorned in tarnished brass, reclaimed furniture, and genuine frontier relics, this room is a Western tale come to life. But hold onto your Stetson, ‘cause there’s more to it than meets the eye. Our buckaroos wrangled together the past and the future to deliver a parlour of interactivity for you to discover. So saunter in and loosen your bolo. We’re getting wild comfy over at The Parlour Room.
 
Technologies used include Arduino microcontrollers, DFRobot MP3 player, Adafruit audio codec, microphones, Neopixel LEDs, DC/AC Voltage Generator, Relay Switches, C++”
