UJECSA is a fun twist on block-stacking games, perfect for ages 6 and up! Pull colored blocks, each worth money, and use them to buy game board sections. Own a section? Earn points when others pull your blocks! Play smart as the tower wobbles—when it collapses, the player with the most points wins. Easy to learn, exciting to play, and full of laughs, UJECSA is perfect for family game nights and friendly competitions!
