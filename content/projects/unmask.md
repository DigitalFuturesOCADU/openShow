"The installation is built off the effects of memory distortion, which is a common effect of depression and anxiety. It causes the person who is experience it to have their memory of events altered, sometimes to the point where the memory is almost destroyed.
The installation conveys this through the use of a DSLR, and a depth camera. The two feeds are both applied to a sphere, with the depth camera controlling the height on the points of the sphere, and the DSLR being the colour of the sphere. The DSLR input is fed through a delay, with the depth occurring in real time. This creates the effect of events and actions in the present altering the appearance of the past."
