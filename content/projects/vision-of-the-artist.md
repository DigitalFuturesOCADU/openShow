‘Vision of the Artist’ is an interactive VR experience that explores VR-assisted creative visualization and storytelling as a method to promote subjective well-being. It explores ways to increase positive affects in individuals of all ages, and is designed for the Oculus Quest using Unity. 

Vision of the Artist paints the player as the hero of their own story, as they embark on a journey of “time-travelling” self-discovery, where they overcome their Dream Eater through practicing self-awareness, creative visualization, and seeing the situation in a positive light.
