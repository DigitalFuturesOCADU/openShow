Visual Narration is a series of 5 interactive art works that co-relate with each other to express a particular journey. The process of experiencing through exploration lead to the path of the art and creating definitive visuals of that form lead to its Narration.

The five works talks about the process of how I felt about exploring the self in a metaphorical way and as it progresses I found a new face for myself – literally. While working on the ideas I learnt that a small change in a mathematical value changed the output so majorly, similarly how the decisions in our daily routine affects us significantly and that particularly became the backbone to the visuals. Though it is quite difficult to assign a particular emotion or a name to the series of this work, but it is what I felt in the present scenario and state of mind.

The series is an interaction based works which is developed using the ml5 &amp;  clmtrackr library in p5.js. ml5 is a body tracking library whereas clm tracks different points of the face. To access different works check out the blog post, click the full screen link from the series to unlock the full potential and allow access to the webcam for the computer to track the body position(mostly face).
