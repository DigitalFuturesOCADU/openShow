The project is an interactive visualization of Sternberg’s Triangular Theory of Love. The goal of the project is to educate the viewers about how there are different kinds of love and how the different components of intimacy, commitment/decision, and passion can work to create those different kinds of love. The aesthetic visuals for each individual component will intertwine with the visuals of the other components to create a larger set of visuals for the different kinds of love. This work was created with TouchDesigner and TouchOSC.
