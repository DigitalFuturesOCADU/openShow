The engagement of images with music in the realm of music visualization is a complex interplay of auditory and visual stimuli that can evoke a heightened perception. In this endeavor, I've developed a music visualization technique that blends audio and visuals, aiming to immerse audiences through a computational approach. My goal was to fuse Persian music and the artistic essence of Persian calligraphy in a model that elevates listener expression and resonates with enthusiasts of these musical genres.
