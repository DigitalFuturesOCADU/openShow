EER (emotionally evoked rooms) is a VR multiplayer game experience featuring 4 distinct rooms connected under one name. Each room, Fear, Rage, Zen, and Surprise, each feature their own themes, mechanics, art styles and colour palettes in order to elicit contrasting emotions from the user depending on the room they choose to enter. Users begin in a hub world where they can select which room they would like to enter, either alone or with a friend. The mechanics across the rooms vary from physical destruction and manipulation of objects, to a visual spectacle taking place through walkways. Drawing inspiration from Plutchik's Wheel of Emotions, each room has a signature colour to go with it's unique theme and mechanics.
