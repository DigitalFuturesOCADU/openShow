Weensy and the Giant is designed to be a multiplayer experience revolving around the core mechanics of growing and shrinking. As you explore the garden, you will discover areas where your size will alter your experience and perception of the space. Enjoy the obstacle courses and games laid out in the area with friends as you uncover all that the garden has to offer!
